
Always close unused ends — otherwise reads/writes can block indefinitely.

Pipe has a limited buffer (~64 KB in Linux).

If reader isn’t ready, writer blocks (and vice versa).

open() on FIFO (created with mkfifo) blocks until both reader and writer are connected.
  - It means reader call open fifo means writer blocks and vice versa.
  - when both reader and writer run parallely means it won't block communication happens.

  If you ever want to prevent blocking (for testing or async code), you can open in non-blocking mode:

  int fd = open("myfifo", O_WRONLY | O_NONBLOCK)
  Then open() will return immediately — but if no reader is connected, write() will fail with ENXIO.
